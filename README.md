Blender to Substance Painter Bridge

Send your model from Blender to Substance Painter with one click,
and get the textures back with one click.

## How to start
1. Click "Blender_Painter_Bridge_v1.0.zip" above, then click the Download button.
2. Unzip it (right click > Extract All).
3. Open the "READ ME FIRST.txt" file inside and follow the easy steps.

Needs: Windows, Blender 4.2 or newer, Substance 3D Painter.
